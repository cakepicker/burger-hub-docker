About Burger Hub

Good food and good times, served fresh.

Burger Hub started with one simple mission: bring mouth-watering, affordable burgers to students, families and burger lovers everywhere. What began as a small corner stall became a community favourite, known for juicy patties, crispy fries and a welcoming vibe.

We believe great food brings people together. Every burger is made fresh, from perfectly seasoned beef and melty cheese to crisp lettuce and soft toasted buns. Whether you are grabbing a bite between classes or hanging out with friends, we have a meal waiting.

Visit us at New Beverly Hills or the Student Boarding House in Cikarang, or order online for delivery.
